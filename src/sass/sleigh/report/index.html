SASS semantic coverage

This report shows which SASS instructions our generated Ghidra languages can model: what an instruction computes and writes to its outputs.

Worked example: reading F2F coverage

The cards below count an opcode as covered when any of its forms has semantics; their percentages do not measure complete support. The report uses the exact floating-point language variants. Generated by sass-refresh-coverage.

Search by opcode name, or choose an evidence source to keep rows with interaction evidence in at least one cell (in the selected architecture’s cell when an architecture filter is active). Sources are independent; “interactions without evidence” finds legal modeled requirements without any positive reference, GPU or PTX evidence. Dimmed cells do not match the evidence filter. Click an architecture heading or card to require that its column contain the opcode; other columns stay visible for comparison. Click again to clear that selection. Category counts follow the visible rows; cards always summarize the full architecture. Hatched cells mean the opcode is absent from that architecture’s machine description.
